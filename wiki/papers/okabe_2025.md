# Okabe et al. (2025)

## One-line Summary
eRASS1 78 团的 HSC 弱透镜质量测量，methodology 参考。

## Sample
78 eRASS1 clusters in HSC-SSP S19A footprint。

## Key Results
- Individual WL mass measurements
- Scaling relations: mass vs richness, mass vs count-rate
- Mass-concentration relation from Bayesian framework
- Miscentering analysis

## Methods
- Instruments: eROSITA + HSC
- Weak lensing without priors on X-ray quantities
- NFW profile fitting
