# Chiu et al. (2022)

## One-line Summary
eFEDS 434 团的 X-ray observable-to-mass 标度关系，首次 HSC 弱透镜质量校准。

## Sample
434 个 eFEDS optically-confirmed 团 (0.01 < z < 1.3, median z=0.35)，其中 313 个有 HSC S19A 弱透镜覆盖。

## Key Results
- Count rate–M500–z relation calibrated with weak lensing
- Additional scaling relations: LX, TX, Mgas, YX vs M500
- Bayesian population modeling with blind analysis
- Mass range: $10^{13}$–$10^{15} h^{-1} M_\odot$

## Methods
- Instruments: eROSITA (X-ray) + HSC (weak lensing)
- Fitting: Bayesian, simultaneously fitting count rate–mass–z and WL mass–mass–z
- Energy band: rest-frame soft band
