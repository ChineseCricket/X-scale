# Ramos-Ceja et al. (2025)

## One-line Summary
eRASS1 西银半球 3061 团的 X 射线标度关系，迄今最大样本。Slopes 偏离 self-similar，evolution 一致。

## Sample
3061 eRASS1 groups/clusters (0.05 < z < 1.07)，$M_{500} = 1.1\times10^{13}$–$1.6\times10^{15} M_\odot$。迄今最大标度关系分析样本。

## Key Results
- LX-T, LX-Mgas, LX-YX, Mgas-T relations
- Slopes: deviate significantly from self-similar
- Redshift evolution: consistent with self-similar
- Small statistical uncertainties from large sample
- Good agreement with previous studies and simulations with non-gravitational physics

## Methods
- Instrument: eROSITA
- Selection function from simulations, rigorously incorporated
- Fitting: Bayesian
- Energy band: 0.2–2.3 keV (soft band)
