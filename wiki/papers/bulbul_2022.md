# Bulbul et al. (2022)

## One-line Summary
eFEDS 团簇群的 X 射线性质和标度关系。LX-T, LX-Mgas 等偏离 self-similar。

## Sample
~542 eFEDS groups and clusters (0.01 < z < 1.3)。eROSITA Final Equatorial-Depth Survey。

## Key Results
- LX-T slope: deviates from self-similar (3/2)
- LX-Mgas, LX-YX, Mgas-T relations
- Slopes deviate from self-similar; redshift evolution consistent with self-similar
- Small statistical uncertainties from large sample

## Methods
- Instrument: eROSITA
- Fitting: Bayesian with selection function
- Energy band: 0.2–2.3 keV (soft band)
